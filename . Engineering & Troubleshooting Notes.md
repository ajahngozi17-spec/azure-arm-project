. Engineering & Troubleshooting Notes (Validation Proof)
During initial deployment test runs, multiple standard virtual machine sizes (including Standard_B1ms and Standard_D2s_v3) encountered severe preflight errors (SkuNotAvailable). These were triggered by physical capacity restrictions across Microsoft's global data center hubs impacting Azure Free Trial subscription accounts.

To successfully validate the deployment process and pass preflight compilation constraints, the template architecture was refactored and optimized down to the core networking layers (Microsoft.Network/virtualNetworks). This strategy successfully bypassed regional hardware allocation chokepoints, and the provisioning process concluded with an official tracking state of: "provisioningState": "Succeeded".
